The past few decades have seen a considerable rise in implementations of a variety of digital interventions to encourage physical activity. Just how effective are they? In fact, can they be considered to be equally effective across groups of varying socioeconomic status? Questions like this are addressed in The effectiveness of digital interventions for increasing physical activity in individuals of low socioeconomic status: a systematic review and meta-analysis, published by Western et al. in the International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity in 2021.

This site provides an overview of this meta-analysis: what was done, what were the main results, as well as an assessment of the overall quality using the M.A.G.I.C. criteria. In addition to this, we have highlighted two sample studies that were included in the meta-analysis, ones that we believe stood out in terms of their study designs and outcomes.
